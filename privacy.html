Privacy

While. · Last updated 15 October 2026

While collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own, and no server to send anything to. The app contains no networking code at all — there is nothing in it that can make a network request.

Everything below is a consequence of that, written out so you can check it.

What the app reads, and where it stays

While asks for some permissions to do its job. Each one is read on your device and stored on your device.

PermissionWhat it is forWhere it goes
Screen Time
(Family Controls)		 Locking and unlocking the apps you choose. Stays on your device. Apple hands While an opaque token for each app you pick — While cannot read which app a token refers to, only lock and unlock it.
Motion & Fitness Counting steps, so the app can tell you are moving. Stays on your device.
Location
(While Using the App)		 Measuring distance during a session, drawing your route afterwards, and keeping the app awake so it notices when you stop. Stays on your device. Routes are saved only for sessions you tracked with Location, and only on your phone.
Health
(heart rate)		 Reading heart rate from an Apple Watch during a session. Read only, never written. Nothing is saved to Health and nothing is stored outside the running session.
Notifications Telling you an app has unlocked while your phone is in your pocket. Composed and delivered on your device.

Your banked time, your workout log, your session history and your settings are stored on your device, in the app's own storage and an app group shared only with While's own Watch app, widgets and Screen Time extension.

What is never collected

No name, no email, no account, no device identifier, no advertising identifier, no contacts, no photos, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry, no location history off the device. While never asks for, stores or displays body metrics — no weight, no calories, no body fat, no before-and-after. That is a product rule, not just a privacy one.

Third parties

There are none. While contains no third-party SDKs, no trackers and no analytics. The exercise photographs in the app are bundled with it, from the public-domain free-exercise-db — they are already on your phone and are never fetched.

Apple

If you install While from TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect its own information about downloads and crashes under Apple's privacy policy. That is between you and Apple; While does not receive it and cannot see it.

Children

While is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone. Screen Time authorization must be granted by an adult account.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it. There is no copy anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If While ever starts collecting anything — it has no plans to — that will be stated here before it ships, not after.
