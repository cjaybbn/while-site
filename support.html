Getting started

  1. Allow Screen Time. It is the only way an app can hold another app shut. It needs an adult Apple Account.
  2. Pick your apps in the Vault.
  3. Choose how each one unlocks — on banked time, or only while you are moving.
  4. Start a session and move.

Common questions

An app won't unlock.

Check the Vault. An app set to While moving only opens during a live session. An app set to Banked time opens while your balance is above zero and re-locks when it runs out.

My session stopped on its own.

While needs a movement signal to stay open. Under Location it holds for about ninety seconds after you stop. Under Phone motion it needs steps, which a bike or a bench press will not produce — pick Location or Apple Watch for those.

Why does it ask for location?

With your phone locked, location updates are the only thing keeping While awake to notice you have stopped moving. "While Using the App" is enough; it runs behind the blue indicator. Nothing leaves your phone.

The Apple Watch app isn't on my wrist.

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, and tap Install next to While. Heart rate is the only feature that needs it — every other signal works without a Watch.

Can I get around it?

Yes. You can turn Screen Time off, or delete the app. While is a speed bump you agreed to, not a lock someone else holds the key to. It is built that way on purpose.

Where is my data?

On your phone. While has no accounts and no servers — see the privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes everything.
