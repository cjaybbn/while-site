While. · Last updated 16 October 2026

The short version: While is an app you install on your own phone to limit your own apps. It is not a medical device, it is not a lock anybody else controls, and you can leave at any time and take your data with you — because it never left.

Who this is between

While is made by Blackburn Creative Studio. Using the app means agreeing to these terms and to Apple's standard End User License Agreement, which applies to every app distributed through the App Store.

What the app does

While uses Apple's Screen Time APIs to shield apps you choose on the device you are holding, and to lift that shield when you have moved. It cannot read which apps those are — Apple hands it an opaque token — and it cannot be used to monitor or restrict anybody else's device.

You can always get out

You can turn Screen Time off, or delete While, and every restriction it applied is released. This is deliberate and will not change. While is a speed bump you agreed to, not a lock someone else holds the key to.

What happens if you stop paying

Nothing is taken from you. Your banked minutes stay yours and your whole record stays yours — you moved for them. Any app beyond the one the free plan holds is unlocked, not kept locked, and the app tells you when that happens. Your choice of apps is remembered, so resubscribing puts everything back as it was.

What While is not

While is not medical or health advice, not a treatment for anything, and not a substitute for a doctor. It records movement you tell it about and time you spend in apps; it does not diagnose, and it never asks for or displays body metrics. Check with a doctor before starting any new exercise.

Your data

It is on your phone. There is no account to close and no copy of anything to request, because there is no server — see the privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
